安装时被系统拦住怎么办？ · The installer is blocked

内测版还没有代码签名。Windows：出现蓝色提示时点「更多信息」→「仍要运行」。Mac：把 Study Hub 拖进「应用程序」后，第一次右键 App →「打开」；如果还被拦，去「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」点「仍要打开」。
The beta isn't code-signed yet. Windows: “More info” → “Run anyway”. Mac: after dragging Study Hub into Applications, right-click the app → Open the first time; if it is still blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.

不连接 Canvas 能先试用吗？ · Can I try it without Canvas?

可以。首次打开时点「先看看演示（示例数据）」，就能用虚构的课程体验全部功能，完全离线。
Yes. On first launch tap “先看看演示（示例数据）” (Try the demo) to explore every screen with fictional courses, fully offline.

怎么连接我的 Canvas？ · How do I connect Canvas?

在连接界面填写学校的 Canvas 网址并登录。如果你的学校还没有开通 Study Hub 的 Canvas 登录，可以在 Canvas「账户 → 设置」里生成个人访问令牌（Access Token）后粘贴。令牌只保存在你设备的钥匙串里。
Enter your institution's Canvas address and sign in. If sign-in is not enabled for your institution yet, create a personal access token in Canvas (Account → Settings) and paste it. The token stays in your device keychain.

考试日期以哪里为准？ · Which date is authoritative?

永远以学校和 Canvas 的原始通知为准。Study Hub 帮你整理和提醒，自动识别的规则会标出原始来源，你可以点进去核对。
Your institution and the original Canvas notice are always authoritative. Detected rules link to their source so you can check.

怎么删除我的数据？ · How do I delete my data?

设置 → 仅本机存储 →「抹除本设备全部 Study Hub 数据」。卸载 App 也会删除所有本机数据。
Settings → Storage → “Erase all Study Hub data on this device”. Deleting the app also removes everything.

Study Hub 是 Canvas 官方的吗？ · Is this an official Canvas app?

不是。Study Hub 是独立开发的第三方学习工具，与 Instructure（Canvas 的开发商）及任何学校没有隶属关系。
No. Study Hub is an independent third-party tool, not affiliated with Instructure (maker of Canvas) or any institution.
