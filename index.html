FAQ

Is this made by my university?这是学校官方做的吗？

No. Study Hub is an independent student project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any university or Instructure (Canvas). Always check the official notice for exam details.不是。Study Hub 是独立的学生项目，与任何大学和 Instructure（Canvas）都没有隶属或背书关系。考试信息请以官方通知为准。

Why do I need to reconnect every 30 days?为什么每 30 天要重新连接？

Many schools make student Canvas tokens expire after 30 days. Study Hub reminds you a few days before so syncing doesn't stop.很多学校规定学生的 Canvas 令牌 30 天过期。Study Hub 会提前几天提醒你更换，同步不会中断。

What if the app reads a date wrong?识别的日期错了怎么办？

Open the item and correct it — your fix stays on your device. Use “Send feedback” in Settings so we can improve detection.点开那一项直接改，改动保存在你的设备上。也欢迎在设置里点「反馈问题」，帮我们改进识别。

Is the AI included? Is it allowed?AI 要钱吗？学校允许用吗？

You connect your own AI account and the provider bills you directly (or run Ollama free on your computer). Chat subscriptions like ChatGPT Plus don't include API access. The assistant explains, quizzes and plans but won't write work you submit; each unit sets its own rules on AI use, so check your unit outline.AI 用的是你自己的账户，费用由服务商直接收取（也可以在自己电脑上免费运行 Ollama）。ChatGPT Plus 这类聊天订阅不包含 API。助手负责讲解、出题和排计划，不会替你写要提交的作业；每门课对 AI 的规定不同，请以 unit outline 为准。
