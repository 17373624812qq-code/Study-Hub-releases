Study Hub 隐私政策

中文English

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-10 · 适用于 Study Hub 7.2 及以后版本

一句话概括

Study Hub 不收集你的任何个人数据。你的课程、作业、考试和邮件内容只保存在你自己的设备上，开发者看不到，也不会上传到任何 Study Hub 服务器。

App 读取哪些内容

  • Canvas：经你授权后，App 直接从你学校的 Canvas 读取课程、作业、考试、日历、公告、课程页面和参考文件。只读，不修改、不提交任何内容。
  • Outlook（可选）：如果你选择连接，App 以只读权限（Mail.Read、User.Read、offline_access）读取收件箱，在设备上筛出课程相关邮件。不发信、不改信、不删信。
  • 你手动添加的内容：你自己添加的重要日期和粘贴保存的邮件文字。

AI 复习（可选，默认关闭）

如果你在「AI 复习」里连接你自己的 AI 服务（例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、DeepSeek、Google Gemini、阿里云通义千问、Moonshot、OpenRouter，或在自己电脑上运行的 Ollama），那么当你提问时，你的问题、AI 为回答而查阅的课程内容（课程页面、作业说明、截止时间、已公布的分数、公告、Modules 目录），以及你自己添加的资料，会从你的设备直接发送给你选择的服务，由该服务按它自己的隐私政策处理并向你计费。这些内容不经过 Study Hub 服务器，开发者看不到。Outlook 邮件不会发给 AI。API 密钥保存在系统钥匙串里。不连接就不会发送任何内容；在设置里可以随时断开。

数据存在哪里

以上内容都只保存在当前设备的 App 数据目录里。登录凭证存放在系统钥匙串或凭证管理器中。每台设备各自直接连接 Canvas 和 Outlook，彼此之间不通过我们同步。

关于 Canvas 一键登录：如果你使用「Canvas 安全登录」，登录授权会经过 Study Hub 的登录中转服务。它替 App 向你学校的 Canvas 换取登录令牌，因为 Canvas 要求这一步使用不能放进 App 的密钥。中转服务只在内存中转发授权码和刷新令牌，不保存它们，也从不接触你的课程、作业或邮件内容。使用访问令牌（Access Token）连接时，不经过中转服务。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集、不出售、不分享你的个人信息或学习内容。
  • 不做广告，不追踪，不使用第三方统计或分析 SDK。
  • 除非你自己连接并使用「AI 复习」，否则不会把你的课程内容发给任何 AI 服务；邮件内容在任何情况下都不会发给 AI。

网络连接

App 只连接你自己学校的 Canvas、Study Hub 登录中转服务（仅在使用 Canvas 一键登录和刷新登录时）、Microsoft 登录与 Microsoft Graph（仅在你连接 Outlook 时）、你自己选择的 AI 服务（仅在你使用 AI 复习时），以及你主动打开的外部链接。演示模式完全离线。

删除你的数据

在 App 的「设置」里可以分别清除离线文件、重置课程缓存、断开 Canvas、断开 Outlook，或「抹除本设备全部 Study Hub 数据」。卸载 App 也会删除这些数据。这些操作只影响本机副本，不会删除 Canvas 或 Outlook 中的原始内容。

儿童

Study Hub 面向大学生，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更与联系

政策如有变更，会在此页面更新并修改生效日期。有疑问请通过 支持页面 联系我们。

In short

Study Hub does not collect any personal data. Your courses, assignments, exams and email content are stored only on your own device. The developer cannot see them and they are never uploaded to a Study Hub server.

What the app reads

  • Canvas: with your permission, the app reads your courses, assignments, exams, calendar, announcements, pages and reference files directly from your institution's Canvas. Read-only; it never changes or submits anything.
  • Outlook (optional): if you connect it, the app uses read-only access (Mail.Read, User.Read, offline_access) to your inbox and filters course-related mail on your device. It never sends, edits or deletes mail.
  • Things you add: important dates you enter and email text you paste in yourself.

AI review (optional, off by default)

If you connect your own AI service in “AI review” (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Alibaba Cloud Qwen, Moonshot, OpenRouter, or Ollama running on your own computer), then when you ask a question, your question, the course content the AI looks up to answer it (pages, assessment instructions, due dates, released marks, announcements, module lists) and files you add yourself are sent directly from your device to the service you chose, which handles them under its own privacy policy and bills you. None of it passes through a Study Hub server and the developer cannot see it. Outlook mail is never sent to the AI. The API key is kept in the system keychain. Nothing is sent unless you connect a service, and you can disconnect it in Settings at any time.

Where data is stored

All of the above stays in the app's data folder on the current device. Sign-in credentials are kept in the system keychain or credential manager. Each device connects to Canvas and Outlook on its own; nothing is synced through us.

Canvas one-tap sign-in: if you use “Canvas secure sign-in”, the sign-in passes through the Study Hub sign-in relay, which exchanges tokens with your institution's Canvas because Canvas requires a secret that cannot be shipped inside the app. The relay forwards authorization codes and refresh tokens in memory only, never stores them, and never sees your courses, assignments or mail. Connecting with an access token does not use the relay.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information or study content.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Your course content is not sent to any AI service unless you connect and use “AI review” yourself; mail content is never sent to an AI.

Network connections

The app connects only to your institution's Canvas, the Study Hub sign-in relay (only for Canvas one-tap sign-in and token refresh), Microsoft sign-in and Microsoft Graph (only if you connect Outlook), the AI service you chose (only when you use AI review), and links you choose to open. Demo mode works fully offline.

Deleting your data

In Settings you can clear offline files, reset the course cache, disconnect Canvas, disconnect Outlook, or erase all Study Hub data on this device. Deleting the app also removes it. These actions only affect the copy on your device; nothing is deleted from Canvas or Outlook.

Children

Study Hub is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date. For questions, use the support page.
